ABOUT BANTAYBARANGAY

Digital public service built around the community.

BantayBarangay is a digital barangay service platform designed to provide residents with a more convenient way to access community services, submit concerns, monitor requests, and interact with barangay processes.

The platform brings important resident services into one organized system while supporting transparency, accountability, accessibility, and secure record management.

DIGITAL GOVERNANCE

One Community. One Digital Portal.

Bringing essential barangay services closer to residents through an organized and accessible digital platform.

OUR PURPOSE

Making barangay services more accessible

BantayBarangay helps connect residents and barangay services through a centralized digital environment.

Accessibility

Give residents convenient access to barangay services, requests, reports, and important information.

Transparency

Help residents follow the status and progress of requests and complaints through organized digital records.

Secure Records

Support controlled access to resident records, submitted documents, complaints, evidence, and barangay transactions.

OUR MISSION

Supporting responsive and transparent barangay governance.

BantayBarangay aims to improve communication between residents and barangay services by providing tools that make common transactions easier to access, monitor, and manage.

Through digital processes, residents can interact with barangay services while officials can maintain more organized records and service workflows.

DIGITAL SERVICES

What BantayBarangay provides

The platform combines several important barangay processes into one resident-accessible system.

01

Complaint Submission

Residents can submit formal complaints and community concerns with supporting information and evidence.

02

Complaint Tracking

Submitted concerns can be monitored as they move through review, investigation, mediation, and resolution processes.

03

Document Requests

Residents can request available barangay documents and monitor their request status.

04

Document Verification

The system provides tools for verifying supported barangay-issued digital documents and records.

05

Resident Verification

Registration information and submitted identity documents can be reviewed as part of the resident verification process.

06

Case & Hearing Management

Barangay personnel can organize case-related hearings, mediation activities, evidence, and supporting records.
